Fatieyc Plugin — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21 · Publisher: Fatih Gülen · Contact: faatihgulen@icloud.com
Applies to: Fatieyc Plugin for AutoCAD 2025 / 2026 / 2027 (all versions)

1. Data we collect

Fatieyc Plugin does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data or usage data. It contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, advertising, third-party SDKs or licensing server, and it makes no network connections.

2. Data stored locally on your computer

The plugin saves the following files only on your own computer. They are never sent to the publisher or anyone else.

File / locationPurpose
%APPDATA%\FatieycPlugin\layer_settings.jsonArea Calculator layer settings
%APPDATA%\FatieycPlugin\explodeview.settingsExploded Layout options
Folder you choose when exportingExcel/CSV, OBJ/MTL and DWG files you export, plus small export/import log files
%TEMP% (fatieyc_*, *_exploded_*.dwg)Temporary helper files
Blender add-ons folder (optional)Blender importer add-on, copied only when you request it

3. Third parties

No data is shared with any third party, including legal affiliates of the publisher (parent, subsidiary or related entities). If this ever changes, any such third party will be required to provide the same protection of the data as stated in this policy, and this policy will be updated before the change takes effect.

4. Data retention and deletion

The publisher holds no user data, so nothing is retained by the publisher. Local files remain on your computer until you delete them. Uninstalling the plugin removes the plugin bundle. You can delete %APPDATA%\FatieycPlugin at any time to remove all saved settings.

5. Revoking consent and requesting deletion

Because no data is collected, there is no consent to revoke and no data held by the publisher to delete. You can stop all local storage by uninstalling the plugin and deleting the folders listed above. For any privacy question or request, email faatihgulen@icloud.com; we reply within 30 days.

6. Changes to this policy

Any change will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

Accessing this policy inside the app

Click Privacy Policy in the top-right of the Fatieyc Plugin window, or type the AutoCAD command FATPRIVACY.
